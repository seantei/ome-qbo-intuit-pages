Privacy Policy

Overview

Invoice Reconciliation Agent is an internal tool used by Organic Milk Exchange and authorized affiliated company users to compare invoice records with QuickBooks Online data. The tool is not offered to the public.

Information The App Accesses

When an authorized user connects a QuickBooks Online company, the app may read accounting data needed for invoice reconciliation, including vendors, bills, purchases, expenses, invoice numbers, dates, amounts, balances, and payment status.

The app does not request or use QuickBooks Payroll, Payments API, bank-feed administration, tax filing, user administration, or payment-method management access.

How Information Is Used

QuickBooks data is used only to determine whether collected invoices appear to be not found, entered, unpaid, paid, duplicated, or in need of manual review. The app does not use QuickBooks data for advertising, sale, resale, profiling, or unrelated analytics.

Storage And Security

OAuth token material is stored in the authorized operator's local macOS Keychain or another approved secure credential store. Reconciliation reports may store non-secret invoice status information, such as matched vendor name, transaction type, transaction ID, amount, and match confidence. Client secrets, access tokens, refresh tokens, passwords, MFA codes, and recovery codes are not stored in reports.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share QuickBooks data with advertisers or data brokers. Reconciliation outputs are used internally by authorized personnel for invoice operations and accounting review.

Retention

Reconciliation reports and operational records are retained only as needed for accounting, audit, invoice workflow, and duplicate-prevention purposes. Access tokens can be revoked by disconnecting the app in QuickBooks Online or by contacting the internal app owner.

Contact

Questions about this internal app may be sent to ar@organicmilkexchange.com.
