Internal QuickBooks Online tool

Invoice Reconciliation Agent

This private app helps authorized Organic Milk Exchange team members compare collected invoice records with QuickBooks Online bills, expenses, vendors, balances, and payment status.

Purpose

The app is used only for read-only invoice reconciliation. It helps avoid duplicate invoice processing by checking whether an invoice appears to have already been entered or paid in an authorized QuickBooks Online company.

Boundaries

  • The app reads QuickBooks accounting data for reconciliation.
  • The app does not create, edit, approve, void, submit, or pay QuickBooks records.
  • The app does not change vendors, bank feeds, payment methods, users, payroll, tax settings, or account settings.
  • Access is limited to authorized internal users and authorized QuickBooks Online companies.
