End User License Agreement

Internal Use Only

Invoice Reconciliation Agent is licensed only for authorized internal use by Organic Milk Exchange and authorized affiliated company users. It is not a public product or marketplace application.

Permitted Use

Authorized users may use the app to connect approved QuickBooks Online company files and compare invoice records against QuickBooks bills, purchases, expenses, vendors, balances, and payment status.

Read-Only Restrictions

The app is intended for read-only reconciliation. Users must not use the app to create, edit, delete, void, approve, submit, or pay QuickBooks records. Users must not use the app to change vendors, payment methods, payroll, bank feeds, account settings, users, taxes, passwords, or MFA settings.

User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for connecting only QuickBooks Online companies they are authorized to access, reviewing reconciliation outputs before taking accounting action, and following company approval procedures before any invoice is paid or sent for payment.

No Payment Authorization

A match or status produced by the app is not payment authorization. Payment approval must be provided through the company's normal approval process.

Termination And Disconnect

Access may be removed at any time by revoking the app connection in QuickBooks Online or by contacting the internal app owner. Disconnection stops future QuickBooks API access for the affected company.
