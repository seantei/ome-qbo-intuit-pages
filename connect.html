QuickBooks Online connection

Connect Or Reconnect

This page is used as the public connect/reconnect location for Invoice Reconciliation Agent. The actual QuickBooks Online authorization flow is started by an authorized internal operator from the invoice automation workspace.

During authorization, Intuit asks the authorized user to select a QuickBooks Online company and approve access. The app requests accounting read access for invoice reconciliation only.

If you are an authorized user and need to connect or reconnect a company, contact ar@organicmilkexchange.com.
